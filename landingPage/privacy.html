Clinic data in the software

Patient and business records entered into DiagnoCare belong to the clinic. We process them only to provide the service, keep each clinic in its own database, and do not use them for any other purpose.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Backups are encrypted. Staff accounts are personal and can use two-step sign-in. Changes to money, results and permissions are recorded in an audit log.

Keeping and deleting data

When a clinic leaves, it can export its data. We delete it after the period agreed in the clinic's contract, apart from backups that expire on their normal schedule.
