The service

DiagnoCare is software for diagnostic centers provided by Softorio as a monthly or yearly subscription.

Payment

Subscriptions are paid in advance. If a payment is late, the software becomes read-only after a grace period: records stay visible and printable, but new entries stop until payment.

Clinical responsibility

DiagnoCare records and prints what the clinic's staff and analyzers enter. Medical decisions and the correctness of reports remain the responsibility of the clinic and its qualified staff.

Your data

The clinic owns its data and can export it at any time.

Accounts

Each person uses their own account and keeps their password secret.
